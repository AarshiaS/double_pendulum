Divergence of A and B

Separation on a log scale. A rising straight line means exponential growth, the signature of a positive Lyapunov exponent.

Model assumptions: rigid massless rods, point masses, frictionless joints, no air resistance, constant g. Potential energy is measured from the lowest configuration, so V = 0 when both bobs hang straight down. Damping adds a torque proportional to each angular velocity.
